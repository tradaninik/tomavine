# Tomavine — Where growers meet gardens

India-first marketplace connecting physically-verified nurseries with home gardeners,
farmers and landscapers: plants, farmer saplings sold by the tray with per-acre
guidance, organic inputs, pots, tools and garden-care subscriptions. Cash on delivery.
Six languages. Hyderabad beta.

## Why

- Gardeners fear killing the plant, overpaying, getting something different from the
  photo, and having nobody to ask.
- Farmers buy saplings by the tray and need planting guidance, not a retail cart.
- Nursery owners are phone-first, busy, and invisible online.

## The trust spine (built)

Every nursery is physically verified before listing. Delivery PIN at the doorstep.
Seller-promised delivery dates with on-time verdicts. Delivery ratings tied to real
orders. One pricing source — the displayed price is always the charged price, rescue
discounts included. Reviews only from delivered orders.

## How it works today

- Storefront in English, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Tamil and Marathi; search; categories
  with subcategories; Rescue Corner for discounted stock that needs a home.
- Cart + checkout with COD (₹25 COD fee, buyer-side); free delivery per nursery above ₹499.
- Care guidance at the cart and on the order page — sunlight, watering, soil — so the
  gardener is never alone when the plant wilts.
- Seller dashboard: catalog with photos, stock quick-actions, order cockpit with
  promised-date quick picks.
- Admin console: onboarding, verification queue, scoped staff roles, full audit trail.
- Onboarding is concierge: every verification visit also photographs inventory and
  captures the first 10–20 listings; the founder hands over the dashboard in a
  15-minute OTP walkthrough.

## Business model (decided)

- Pilot: ₹0 commission for the first ~60 days / ~20 nurseries (Hyderabad & surroundings).
- Then: 10% commission on retail orders; 5% on bulk/tray orders of ₹10,000 and above.
- ₹25 COD fee, buyer-side.
- Coming: garden-care subscriptions as the recurring-revenue loop.

## Roadmap snapshot

Product photos (in flight) -> instant seller alerts (Telegram, then WhatsApp) ->
quick re-ordering -> subscriptions + PlantJournal -> soil profiles for accurate bulk
quotes -> weather-aware care nudges.
